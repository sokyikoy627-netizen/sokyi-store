About Sokyi Shop

Two rituals, one small studio.

Sokyi Shop started as a flower stall run out of a single teal-painted cart. Customers kept asking for something to take care of their skin the same way they took care of a fresh bouquet — so we started making that too.

Our story

Grown from a single flower cart

Every arrangement is cut and tied by hand the morning it ships. Every skincare formula is small-batch, cruelty-free, and made with the same flowers we sell at the counter.

What began as a weekend flower stall has grown into a full studio — but the way we work hasn't changed. Small batches, honest ingredients, and care in every box that leaves our door.

  • Hand-tied same-day arrangements
  • Small-batch, cruelty-free skincare
  • Flower-derived ingredients grown in-house
